# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary user is the Kreditmx administrator. The administrator works from the internal panel to issue new credits and keep registered clients, active clients, and new loans organized.

Applicants are secondary users who complete the client registration flow through a private link generated by the administrator.

## Product Purpose

Kreditmx centralizes client registration, credit creation, payment tracking, and investment-fund administration. Success means the administrator can create credits quickly while retaining a clear, reliable view of every client and active loan.

## Operating Context

- The administrator signs in to an internal web panel.
- The administrator generates registration links, reviews applicants, creates and updates loans, records payments, and monitors the investment fund.
- Applicants submit personal, banking, and identification information through a separate registration flow.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing programming logic, functions, calculations, percentages, API contracts, and system behavior must remain unchanged during the visual redesign.
- The current Flask routes and DOM identifiers used by the JavaScript integration are behavioral contracts.
- The visual implementation may change templates, layout, typography, color, spacing, responsive behavior, and presentation-layer styling.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Kreditmx.
- The Figma Make file `SnDNrlG440K39P1zSG6fnG` is the approved visual reference for the redesign.
- Its visual language uses editorial financial typography, an ink-and-paper foundation, acid green and orange accents, fine rules, compact monospace metadata, and dense but structured operational layouts.

## Evidence on Hand

- `templates/admin.html` contains the administrator workflows and current DOM contracts.
- `templates/registro.html` contains the applicant registration workflow.
- `templates/login.html`, `templates/registro_admin.html`, and `templates/cliente.html` contain supporting authentication and client-detail surfaces.
- `js/admin.js` contains the current client, loan, payment, and fund behaviors.
- `app.py` contains the current backend routes, calculations, and persistence behavior.
- The supplied Figma Make source contains dashboard, new-credit, and applicant-registration visual references.

## Product Principles

1. Preserve financial correctness before visual novelty.
2. Make issuing a new credit the clearest primary action.
3. Keep registered clients, active clients, and loans easy to scan and distinguish.
4. Preserve complete operational capability while reducing visual clutter.
5. Keep critical financial states legible on desktop and mobile.
