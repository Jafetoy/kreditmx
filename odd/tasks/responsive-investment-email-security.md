# Responsive, investment concealment, client emails and logout security

Objective: Make every Kreditmx screen work on mobile and desktop, remove investment/earnings from all screens while preserving backend logic, email the client on registration completion and on every credit status change, and prevent browser Back from restoring the admin panel after logout.
Branch: `figma-visual-redesign`. Delivery strategy: `ask-on-risk`; no push, PR or merge is authorized. Test runner: `python -m unittest discover -s tests -v`, `node --check js/admin.js`, Flask route smoke checks and Chromium DOM checks at 1440px and 390px. Forecast: approximately 450 authored changed lines; no commits are made because `app.py` and `database.py` carry the user's own uncommitted work.

## Specs

S1. Make the whole application responsive for phones and desktops, verbatim: "ahora que sea responsivo para dispotivos moviles y pc." Acceptance: the three dashboard panels, tables, forms and headers render without horizontal overflow at 1440px and 390px; mobile ledger rows keep readable column labels.

S2. Remove investment and earnings from every screen, verbatim: "Tambien borra todo lo que sea de inversión, no quiero que aparezca nada de dinero invertido, ni ganancias." Accepted scope, user-selected: "Solo ocultarlo de las pantallas (Recomendado)" — hide the Fondo rail action, the "Capital en movimiento" summary and the whole investment module from the UI, while preserving all endpoints, database tables and payment-distribution calculations. No invested-money or earnings amount may appear in any rendered screen.

S3. Email the client when registration finishes, verbatim: "Ocupo que verifiques que al terminar el registro de los datos en el formulario le llegue un correo al cliente, que sus datos se estan procesando." Acceptance: a successful `/api/registro` sends the existing PENDIENTE notification to the newly created client, best-effort, without breaking the registration response.

S4. Keep the client informed on every credit state, verbatim: "Al igual cuando se apruebe el credito o se rechace o este en espera, lo mantega avisado en que proceso va su credito." Acceptance: PENDIENTE, EN_REVISION, APROBADO and RECHAZADO changes send the matching existing notification; verified by tests because live mail must not be sent to real clients during QA.

S5. Secure logout and Back navigation, verbatim: "Al igual arregla las pantallas que al cerrar sesión y regresar no me tiene que volver al panel sino salir normal, o sea checa esos niveles de seguridad." Acceptance: after logout, Back or bfcache restore of `/admin` or `/cliente` cannot show the panel; protected responses carry no-store headers; the session cookie uses HttpOnly and SameSite; a client-side session re-check redirects to `/login`.

S6. Preserve the existing system contract: all endpoints, calculations, percentages, DOM IDs used by scripts, form field names and business rules stay intact except where S2 explicitly requires hiding an investment screen. The user's uncommitted changes in `app.py` and `database.py` must be preserved.
